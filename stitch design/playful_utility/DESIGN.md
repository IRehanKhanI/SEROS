## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies "Playful Utility"—a dependable, warm, and approachable everyday tool that replaces cold industrial control consoles with clarity and optimism. Designed to empower a diverse spectrum of users—from older adults and individuals with low reading confidence to school administrators and authorized diagnostic technicians—it eliminates technical friction and cognitive fatigue. 

The core operating mantra is: **Make the next useful action obvious.** The system prioritizes immediate utility, plain language, and transparent system status over dense metrics or obscure configuration menus.

### Emotional Response
The interface evokes:
- **Calm Reassurance:** High-contrast legibility, warm earthen canvas backings, and explicit state confirmations reassure users that the system is functioning reliably.
- **Friendly Confidence:** Generously rounded touch surfaces and clear action verbs encourage experimentation without fear of irreversible errors.
- **Dignified Accessibility:** Accessible design choices (such as 48px+ targets and dual icon-label signifiers) are woven natively into the aesthetic rather than isolated as clinical accessibility modes.

### Visual Style
The design style mixes **Modern Utility** with **Soft Tactility**:
- Crisp, card-based surfaces set against a soothing, warm cream background rather than sterile white voids or harsh dark themes.
- Subdued, friendly rounded structures that visually convey softness and approachability.
- Purposeful, restrained color bursts (forest greens, warm peaches, and soft lime highlights) that signify status without visual clutter.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
The palette avoids clinical grays and pure black, grounding the visual environment in an organic "Fresh Garden" spectrum. The canvas background (`#F7F4E8`) provides warm, glare-free comfort for prolonged viewing, while cards and interactive layers elevate using pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or contextual soft pastel tints.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary Green (`#27834A`):** Anchors primary calls-to-action, confirmed operational states, and selected navigation destinations. Communicates positive stability.
- **Secondary Teal (`#276B8C`):** Designated for neutral informational indicators, live streaming tags, and contextual guidance.
- **Tertiary Warm Orange (`#FFB547`):** Serves as an accent highlight to draw focus to opportunities, energy tips, and advisory badges. Never used as a standalone critical alert.
- **Neutral Dark Moss (`#26372D`):** Replaces pure black for headings and body typography, offering deep contrast (exceeding WCAG AAA on canvas and surfaces) with an organic tone.

### Tinted Surfaces & Semantic Feedback
- **`surface-green` (`#EAF5DD`):** Applied to active toggles, verified summaries, and selected list states.
- **`surface-peach` (`#FFF0E4`):** Soft framing for smart tips and contextual helpers.
- **`surface-yellow` (`#FFF4CC`):** Framed notice container for non-blocking alerts or scheduled maintenance notices.
- **`danger` (`#B93832`) & `danger-surface` (`#FCE8E6`):** Reserved strictly for offline hardware, critical system faults, and destructive deletion dialogues.

### Accessibility Safeguards
Every status state must pair a semantic color with a visible icon and text label (e.g., green dot + "Connected", red triangle + "Needs attention"). Color is never used as the single signifier of truth.

## Typography

### Structural Guidance
Typography is powered by `Nunito Sans`, chosen for its open counters, humanist warmth, and rounded terminals. These attributes soften dense technical telemetry into easily readable information for older readers or users navigating visual fatigue.

### Scaling and Readability Principles
- **No All-Caps:** All-caps strings are explicitly prohibited across badges, headings, and labels. Mixed title case or sentence case preserve recognizable silhouette patterns essential for low-literacy readers.
- **Minimum Thresholds:** Critical text never drops below `14px` (`body-sm` / `label-md`). Sub-12px styles are omitted from this design system.
- **High-Contrast Metrics:** Numerical values on dashboard summaries use `metric-display` (`36px`, bold 800) paired with an explicit, plain-language description placed below rather than a complex formulaic superscript.
- **Dynamic Text Support:** Line heights maintain a minimum of 1.4× to 1.5× the font size to allow clean wrapping when system accessibility text-scaling preferences are enlarged up to 200%.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
The design system employs a content-first responsive grid:
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column layout with a persistent bottom navigation bar. Content cards stack vertically to eliminate horizontal tracking. Gutter and margin default to `1rem` (16px), giving maximum surface area to interactive cards.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 2-column modular flow with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Camera status modules and occupancy tallies pair alongside device rosters.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Fixed 280px left navigation rail anchored beside a 12-column adaptive workspace. The maximum container width is capped at `1280px` to maintain comfortable eye-scanning spans.

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing relies on an intuitive 8px/4px base scale:
- `space-xs` (8px): Inline distance between icons and their adjacent descriptive labels.
- `space-sm` (12px): Internal padding inside compact pills, chips, and segmented indicators.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard gap between form fields and standard component padding.
- `space-lg` (24px): Interior padding for hero summary cards and section gaps on tablet/desktop.
- `space-xl` (32px): Separation between distinct workflow sections (e.g., between Live Occupancy and Scheduled Automations).

## Elevation & Depth

### Ambient Soft Depth
To avoid visual noise, this design system rejects heavy, sharp dropshadows and frosted glass blurs in favor of **Tonal Layers with Ambient Tinted Depth**:

1. **Base Layer (Canvas):** The warm background (`#F7F4E8`) serves as ground level 0.
2. **Elevated Surfaces (Level 1 Cards):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards resting on the canvas, bounded by a `1.5px` border of `#E3E5D8` and an ambient, warm diffused shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(38, 55, 45, 0.04)`.
3. **Interactive Hover / Focused State (Level 2):** Elevated cards gain subtle kinetic lift: `0 8px 24px rgba(38, 55, 45, 0.08)` while maintaining their structural border.
4. **Modals & Bottom Drawers (Level 3):** Grounded by a warm dimmed backdrop overlay (`rgba(38, 55, 45, 0.40)`) with an elevated surface shadow: `0 16px 36px rgba(38, 55, 45, 0.14)`.

### Border Integrity
Every card, form container, and interactive chip possesses a visible border (`#E3E5D8`). This structural contour provides distinct boundary clarity for users with diminished contrast perception, ensuring cards never bleed invisibly into the canvas.

## Shapes

### Pill & Ultra-Soft Geometry
The design system adopts roundedness level `3` (Pill-shaped profile), reinforcing an inviting, tactile look:
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** Utilize a full pill shape (`roundedness: 3`, minimum border-radius of `1rem` up to `9999px`), ensuring comfortable touch targets without sharp, intimidating corners.
- **Main Content Cards:** Border radius set to `1.25rem` – `1.5rem` (20px – 24px) to create soft frames for data dashboards and device lists.
- **Surface Badges & Chips:** Rendered as full pills (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish contextual status tokens from rectangular content wrappers.
- **Modals & Drawers:** Feature generous top radii of `2rem` (32px), creating a friendly presentation when unfolding from the screen base.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Touch Targets
- **Sizing:** All interactive touch surfaces strictly maintain a minimum height and width of `48px` × `48px` (often padded out to `52px` on mobile), accommodating tremors or low dexterity.
- **Primary Button:** Solid Forest Green (`#27834A`) fill, text in `#FFFFFF` with `label-lg`, rounded-full (`9999px`). Pressed state deepens to `#1F693B`. Every primary button pairs an intuitive action verb ("Turn off lights", "View room") alongside an SVG icon.
- **Secondary Button:** White (`#FFFFFF`) surface, `1.5px` border in `#27834A`, text in `#27834A`. Hover/focus adopts `#EAF5DD`.
- **Destructive Button:** Crimson Red (`#B93832`) border or subtle fill with `#B93832` text, requiring explicit confirmation modal dialogs before taking effect.

### Cards & Surface Modules
- Constructed on a `#FFFFFF` base with `#E3E5D8` boundary line and `20px` corner radii.
- Internal padding is locked to `20px` (mobile) and `24px` (desktop).
- **Rule of One:** Each card handles exactly one topic (e.g., "Room 102 Occupancy" or "Today's Power Use"). Deeply nested sub-cards are prohibited to keep information hierarchies flat.

### Status Chips & Badges
- High-contrast pill components containing an explicit SVG icon + text label.
  - *Active / Connected:* `#EAF5DD` background with `#27834A` text and filled checkmark icon.
  - *Attention / Scheduled:* `#FFF4CC` background with `#26372D` text and clock/alert icon.
  - *Offline / Error:* `#FCE8E6` background with `#B93832` text and warning diamond icon.
  - *Demo / Sample Data:* Subdued neutral tint with explicit "Sample Data" label.

### Input Fields & Forms
- Always provide an explicit, persistent label rendered above the field in `label-md` (`#26372D`). Placeholders must never double as labels.
- Minimum input height of `50px`, styled with `#FFFFFF` background, `#E3E5D8` border, and `16px` internal padding.
- Focused inputs receive a `2px` ring in `#27834A` with zero layout shift.
- Validation hints render inline below the field using short, helpful instructions (e.g., "Enter a room name like 'Science Lab'").

### Navigation Elements
- **Mobile Bottom Bar:** 5 fixed actions (Home, Rooms, Energy, Devices, More). Fixed `64px` height with labels and icons visible at all times. The active item is indicated by a Forest Green icon and text seated within a soft `#EAF5DD` pill indicator.
- **Desktop Sidebar:** Clean vertical layout grouping items into three plain-language tiers: *Everyday*, *Manage*, and *Admin & Testing*.

### Specialized Components
- **Rule Builder (Automation):** Block cards using "WHEN" and "THEN" visual badges with plain-language action toggles.
- **Camera Feeds:** Fixed-ratio media containers featuring real-time state badges in the top-left corner ("Live", "Reconnecting", or "Offline") to prevent outdated footage from masquerading as real-time activity.
